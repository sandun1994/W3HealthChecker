# Report Builder & Export Architecture

## 1. Overview
The W3HealthChecker Report Builder enables personalized and white-labeled health reporting for website owners, technical developers, and digital marketing agencies.

## 2. Modular Report Sections
Reports are composed of discrete, toggleable sections:
1. **Executive Summary**: High-level overall score (0–100), key takeaways, and critical vulnerability counts.
2. **Pillars Breakdown**:
   - Search Engine Optimization (SEO)
   - Application Security & Encryption
   - Performance & Resource Footprint
   - Accessibility & WCAG Foundations
   - Mobile Responsiveness & Viewports
   - Technical & Structural Infrastructure
   - AI / Search Readiness & Machine Understandability
3. **Prioritized Action Plan**: Ranked list of detected issues sorted by severity (`critical` > `high` > `medium` > `low`) with concrete fix guidance.
4. **Historical Score Trajectory**: Progress delta charts tracking improvements or regressions over monitoring intervals.
5. **Technical Deep-Dive**: Detected technology stack, third-party dependency footprint, and crawl structure.

## 3. Agency White-Label Customization
Agency tier accounts can customize outputs with:
- Agency Name & Contact Information
- Custom Brand Logo URL
- Custom Accent Color & Header Style
- Client Name & Project Association
- Custom Confidentiality or Copyright Footer

Reports maintain ethical integrity: white-label branding does not alter underlying factual scanner signals or forge certifications.
