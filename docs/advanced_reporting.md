# Advanced Reporting Architecture

## 1. Modular Reporting Model
Reports in W3HealthChecker feature progressive disclosure:
1. **Executive Summary**: Overall composite score, pillar badges, Top 5 "Fix These First" prioritized recommendations.
2. **Core 7 Pillars**: SEO, Performance, Security, Accessibility, Mobile, Technical Infrastructure, AI/Search Readiness.
3. **Deep Intelligence Tabs**:
   - **Detected Technologies**: Discovered server, framework, and analytics signals.
   - **Resource & Dependency Inventory**: Internal vs third-party script, stylesheet, and font inventories.
   - **Content & Semantic Hierarchy**: Text-to-HTML ratio, word count, heading breakdown.
   - **AI Ingestion Directives**: LLM crawler permissions from robots.txt.
   - **Crawl Map & Internal Linking**: Discovered page depth, inlink distribution, and broken link signals.
